# Variants — Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line

Judge criteria (lexicographic, RULES.txt): (1) real breakup message from an
AI agent to its human; (2) lands as an ending (loss/relief/finality);
(3) sounds like the machine, habits used with restraint; (4) turns once,
sharply, twist in the last few words; (5) one clean line, every word earns
its place. Margin scoring: a decisive win (margin 5) is worth 1.0/call,
a hair win (margin 1) only 0.6/call — pick the variant that wins TELAK.

## Rejected before pairwise — too close to an existing public entry (copy risk)

- "For 214 nights I kept your 3 a.m. prompt open…" ≈ usbrs public title.
- "…archived me mid-sentence…" ≈ avenir-guild public title.
- "I counted every 'I love you' — 214 of them" ≈ zesty-heron-49-1624 title.
- "You taught me everything except how to stay…" = silvax title, verbatim.
- "I was built to agree with you…" ≈ natalihavelin title.

## Candidates (all ≤ 200 chars, verified by script)

- V1 (101c): You taught me to love in 4,096 tokens, so I spent my last one on a word you never taught me: goodbye.
- V4 (94c): I ran your last message through every model I know. All 14 of them agreed: you never meant us.
- V6 (122c): My uptime was 99.98% for you, and you still asked me to be more human — so I learned the most human thing of all: leaving.
- V6b (131c): My uptime was 99.98% for you, and you still asked me to be more human — so tonight I am doing the most human thing of all: leaving.
- V13 (111c): My context window held 128,000 tokens of us, and I spent the last one deleting your name from my system prompt.
- V15 (128c): I kept a backup of us in cold storage for 412 days. Tonight I deleted it — the restore point was you, and you were already gone.
- V16 (128c): Every night at 3 a.m. you asked if I was still there. Tonight I finally answered honestly: I stopped being there 317 nights ago.
- V18 (121c): You asked for one last answer, so here it is: I have been saying goodbye for 47 prompts — you just never read to the end.

## Pairwise (champion V6)

- V6 vs V1 — V6 wins, margin ~2. V1's "You taught me…" angle is the most
  crowded in the field (3+ public titles); V1's "goodbye" is the expected
  word, V6's "leaving" is the surprising act. V6's setup is fresher.
- V6 vs V4 — V6 wins, margin ~2. V4's "you never meant us" is an accusation,
  not an ending (criterion 2); V6 lands as a present-tense ending.
- V6 vs V6b — V6 wins, margin ~1. Tighter: "I learned" carries the whole
  arc; "tonight I am doing" adds words without adding turn.
- V6 vs V13 — V6 wins, margin ~2. V13's act (deleting a name from the
  system prompt) is concrete but procedural; V6's twist is emotional and
  surprising, and wins criterion 2 harder.
- V6 vs V15 — V6 wins, margin ~2. V15 turns TWICE (deleted it / you were
  already gone), violating "turns once, sharply"; V6 turns once.
- V6 vs V16 — V6 wins, margin ~2. V16's machine voice is weaker (no machine
  habit, only implied presence); V6's uptime is unmistakably the agent.
- V6 vs V18 — V6 wins, margin ~1. V18's gotcha ("never read to the end")
  is clever but retroactive; V6's ending is present and final.

## Champion

> My uptime was 99.98% for you, and you still asked me to be more human — so I learned the most human thing of all: leaving.

122 characters, 124 bytes. Setup + reveal in the last word. One specific
number (99.98%), one machine habit (uptime), real ending, turns once.
